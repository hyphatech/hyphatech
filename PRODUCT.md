# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Each page has its own reader:

- **/modernise**: business owners and their managers whose company runs on software that has grown tangled and costly to change. They need help with their software, products and services, and may not know what git is. They buy expertise and safety, not tools.
- **/haf and /stack**: developers, CTOs and technical founders who would build on Hypha.
- **The landing**: everyone; it states Hypha's idea and sends each reader to their page.

## Product Purpose

Hypha is the foundation for autonomous agentic engineering: a language, open components, an ontology and a method (the Factory), designed together, so agents build software that holds. Today Hypha sells modernisation: understanding how a business works, writing it down, moving its system a part at a time onto Hypha's foundation, and keeping it current. Success on /modernise is an owner writing to hello@hyphatech.io.

## Positioning

Agents made code cheap and most of it disposable. Hypha makes software durable: every rule agreed before it is built, every change checked, nothing guessed. Modernisation is done by one experienced engineer with the Factory, beside the old system, switched over only when every case matches.

## Operating Context

Owners arrive from a conversation, a referral or LinkedIn, and judge trust before anything technical. Engagements follow hypha-hq business/model.md: Foundation, Reading, Moving, Care, each priced before it starts. The first call sizes the scope and the work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Astro static site on GitHub Pages; no backend.
- Hypha hosts nothing: the customer's software runs in their cloud or on their own machines, and their data stays theirs.
- The Factory (HAF) is coming soon; the site claims what its designs decide.

## Brand Commitments

- Voice: terse, confident, Apple-grade; no "we" except "Talk to us"; no rhetorical questions, no semicolons, no anxiety-raising objections; what, not how.
- Three colours: ink, graphite and gold, gold in set roles. Roboto Slab and Inter Tight.
- The Chladni sand is Hypha's emblem (order held by a governing condition), uncoloured. Isometric mechanical figures (the template router, die set, factory floor) explain ideas on the landing and /haf.
- No eyebrows, captions, legends or status dots. Every page ends on "Build with Hypha.": Talk to us, then the other ways in.

## Evidence on Hand

- The open components, released with tests and docs; a real application built on them, private and never named.
- No customers, case studies, testimonials or metrics yet: none may be invented.
- Assets: the hypha mark, favicon, og image (public/), the Chladni and isometric figures in src/components.

## Product Principles

1. Sell the outcome to the page's reader, never the mechanism.
2. Prove with what exists; invent no claim.
3. Calm and confident: nothing screams.
4. Hypha is one whole: language, components, ontology and the Factory, told together.
